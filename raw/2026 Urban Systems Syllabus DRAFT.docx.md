**Course Description:**

Urban Systems explores cities as complex, interconnected networks where physical infrastructure, social dynamics, and technology converge to shape modern urban life. This course examines the city as a "system of systems," where the interaction between diverse elements—from transportation networks to cultural institutions—creates emergent patterns that define today’s urban experiences and challenges.

Students will develop a comprehensive understanding of both **hard systems** (infrastructure, transportation, water, energy, waste) and **soft systems** (social networks, economic development, cultural production, governance) that make cities function. Through a blend of theoretical frameworks and hands-on analysis, students gain expertise in:

* **Systems thinking** applied to urban environments  
* **Data-driven urban analysis** using current NYC datasets  
* **Mapping and modeling** complex urban interactions  
* **Technology applications** for urban challenges (IoT, AI, data science)  
* **Policy and planning** implications of systems approaches

Assignments center on New York City, providing concrete examples of urban systems in action. You'll work with actual city data from NYC OpenData to analyze everything from subway flows to cultural institution distributions.

Guest lectures from leading urban practitioners from government and industry.

**Course Requirements**

The course emphasizes **applied learning** through practical analysis and creative problem-solving that mirrors real-world urban planning and technology work. Rather than traditional exams, students demonstrate mastery through progressive skill-building that culminates in professional-quality deliverables suitable for portfolios. Course requirements include:

* **Weekly Readings:** Expect 2-3 hours of reading per week from seminal urban theory texts and contemporary research papers  
* **Urban Systems Labs:** Student-led interactive sessions for hands-on work with data and collaborative discussions  
* **Project Work:** Significant time investment in mapping, analysis, and multimedia creation using tools like ArcGIS StoryMaps

## **Student Outcomes**

1. **Systems Analysis & Representation:** Demonstrate an ability to recognize, analyze and represent urban systems; understand the relationship between systems and associated complexities.  
2. **"System of Systems" Thinking:** Develop a "System of Systems" sensibility and the ability to address complex urban problems, clearly communicate new concepts, and identify where urban technologies may lead to new solutions.  
3. **Urban Systems Modeling:** Demonstrate practical knowledge of how to model urban systems to support effective simulations of interventions.  
4. **Optimization for Urban Systems:** Explain how to best utilize technology to optimize urban systems, enhance urban resiliency and improve the quality of life for residents.

## **AI Course Policy**

You may use generative AI tools to help with assignments, with some conditions:

You **may** **not** use these tools to answer questions in discussions \- we want *your* thoughts

You **may** use these tools to:

* to research new topics, since it will broaden possibilities  
* to brainstorm different approaches and counters to your arguments  
* to get comprehensive summaries of longer articles you are asked to read so you can take part in discussions  
* to rephrase drafts, improve sentence flow, and generate topic outlines.  
* to produce slides or videos originating from your own source materials

The major challenge for us as communicators is to have something interesting to say and to say it well. Chat GPT is a way to build an interesting infrastructure- how you explain, persuade or inspire is up to you.
